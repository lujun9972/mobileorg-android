Statistics

Overview and Entry

Tap the Statistics item in the main menu to view your pomodoro stats. The Overview tab shows total pomodoros and total duration for today/this week/this month, with a time range selector (day/week/month toggle).

Trend Chart

The Trend tab displays a line chart visualizing daily pomodoro count trends. The chart updates dynamically based on the selected time granularity (day/week/month) to help review long-term focus patterns.

Daily Details

Tap any day in the bar chart on the Overview tab to view that day's full list of pomodoro sessions (each showing start time and duration). Useful for checking work records on specific dates.
